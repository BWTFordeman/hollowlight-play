# Hollowlight

A little autumn world with eight playful activities, original procedural art and sound, and rooms for up to four friends. Enter **Afterdark** through the village for a second, darker survival adventure.

[Play Hollowlight](https://bwtfordeman.github.io/hollowlight-play/)

Desktop browser. WASD or arrows to wander, E to interact, Space to play, M for the journal. Use Bring a friend to share an invitation. The host must keep the game open and visible. Progress lasts for the current visit.

[Play Hollowlight: Afterdark](https://bwtfordeman.github.io/hollowlight-play/?game=afterdark)

Explore five districts of the Old Borough. Survive zombie waves, buy five different guns and a sword, repair barricades, restore power, upgrade at the Emberforge, distract the horde with a clockwork lure, and challenge the Bellkeeper in the sealed chapel. Solo or up to four friends in a separate expedition room.

Afterdark: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left click to fire, R to reload, E to buy/use/repair, F or Space to bash, Q to swap guns, V for the sword, Escape for settings. The host must keep the game open and visible; free peer connections may be unavailable on some restricted networks.

This repository contains the compiled website only. Development source is maintained privately. Browser-delivered assets remain downloadable. Third-party notices are included in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
